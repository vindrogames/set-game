# Privacy Policy for Set Game

**Last updated**: October 12, 2025

## Overview
Set Game is a reskin of the game Quattro. Your privacy is important to us.

## Data Collection
This app does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

## Local Data Storage
- Game scores are stored locally on your device only
- No data is sent to external servers
- No analytics or tracking
- No advertisements
- No third-party services

## Data You Control
- All saved scores are stored in your device's internal storage
- You can delete all data using the "RESET" button in the Rankings screen
- Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

## Children's Privacy
This app does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Check this page for updates.

## Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/pijamarda/PuntosAgricola

## Permissions
This app does not require any special Android permissions.

## Data Security
Since all data is stored locally on your device and no data is transmitted, your scores are as secure as your device itself.
